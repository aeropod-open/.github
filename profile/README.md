# AeroPod Open 🌱

**Open-source AI + IoT precision farming for education, research and sustainable urban agriculture.**

AeroPod Open is an initiative by **AeroGreen Asia Sdn Bhd, Malaysia**.

Our goal is to create an open platform where students, developers, educators, researchers and growers can learn, experiment and build new precision-farming technologies.

## What We Are Exploring

- Open precision-farming APIs
- IoT sensor integrations
- ESP32 and Arduino projects
- Hydroponic automation
- Water and environmental monitoring
- AI and crop-data analysis
- Student and university research
- Community-developed hardware and software

## Get Involved

AeroPod Open is currently forming its founding contributor community.

Whether you are a developer, student, researcher, educator, grower or open-source enthusiast, you are welcome to participate.

👉 Visit the **aeropod-open** repository to explore the roadmap and join our GitHub Discussions.
